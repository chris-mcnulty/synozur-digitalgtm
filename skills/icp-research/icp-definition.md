## Scoring rubric

- **9–10**: Multiple high-value signals in the last 14 days, role and
  company size dead-center. Lead today.
- **7–8**: One strong signal, good fit on role and size. Lead this week.
- **5–6**: General fit, no recent signal. Consider for nurture, not
  cold outreach.
- **3–4**: Marginal fit. Skip for now.
- **1–2**: Wrong role, wrong size, or wrong industry. `disqualified`.
- **0**: Hits a hard disqualifier from `disqualifiers.md`.
